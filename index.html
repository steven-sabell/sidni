About SIDNI

```

SIDNI by Steven Joseph Sabell is a real estate listing inquiry assistant for inbound property inquiries. SIDNI helps consumers submit property questions, showing requests, callback requests, and related contact details to the listing agent for follow-up.

SIDNI is designed for transactional property-inquiry communication. SIDNI does not send cold outreach, purchased-list messaging, unrelated marketing blasts, third-party promotional messages, or loan/financing offers.

SIDNI does not confirm showing appointments. Final showing confirmation must come from the listing agent.

Property Inquiry and SMS Opt-In

Consumers can submit a property inquiry, showing request, or callback request through the public SIDNI property inquiry form.

Users opt in to receive transactional SMS messages from SIDNI by Steven Joseph Sabell only by submitting the public property inquiry form, providing their mobile phone number, and actively selecting the SMS consent checkbox.

The SMS consent checkbox is separate from the rest of the form and is not checked by default. Users must actively select the checkbox before submitting their mobile phone number for SMS follow-up.

Open the Property Inquiry Form and SMS Opt-In

SMS Messaging Summary

Users who opt in may receive transactional SMS messages related to their submitted property inquiry, showing request, callback request, customer-care request, or related follow-up.

  • Message frequency varies based on the user's request and related follow-up.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Users may opt out at any time by replying STOP.
  • Users may request help by replying HELP.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchasing any property, product, or service.

SIDNI does not use text-to-join or keyword-based SMS opt-in for this campaign. The only SMS opt-in method is the public property inquiry form with the unchecked SMS consent checkbox.

Mobile Number Privacy

SIDNI does not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
```
