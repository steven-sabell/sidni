Overview

```

This Privacy Policy explains how SIDNI by Steven Joseph Sabell collects, uses, and protects information submitted through the SIDNI real estate listing inquiry assistant and related public property inquiry form.

SIDNI is a real estate listing inquiry assistant for inbound property inquiries. SIDNI helps consumers submit property questions, showing requests, callback requests, and related contact details to the listing agent for follow-up.

SIDNI does not confirm showing appointments. Final showing confirmation must come from the listing agent.

Information We Collect

SIDNI may collect information that users submit through the public property inquiry form, including:

  • Name.
  • Mobile phone number.
  • Email address, if provided.
  • Property of interest.
  • Request type, such as property inquiry, showing request, or callback request.
  • Message or request details.
  • Requested showing time or callback preference, if provided.
  • SMS consent status when the optional SMS consent checkbox is selected.

SIDNI may also collect basic technical information related to service operation, such as timestamps, request logs, delivery status, and related system records.

How We Use Information

Information submitted to SIDNI is used to:

  • Receive and process property inquiries.
  • Receive and process showing requests.
  • Receive and process callback requests.
  • Route requests and contact details to the listing agent for follow-up.
  • Send transactional SMS messages to users who have opted in to SMS follow-up.
  • Respond to customer-care or support requests.
  • Maintain service records, troubleshooting logs, and operational records.

SIDNI does not use submitted mobile phone numbers for cold outreach, purchased-list messaging, unrelated marketing blasts, third-party promotional messages, or loan/financing offers.

SMS Communications and Consent

You may consent to receive transactional SMS messages from SIDNI by Steven Joseph Sabell by submitting the public SIDNI property inquiry form, providing your mobile phone number, and separately selecting the optional SMS consent checkbox.

The SMS consent checkbox is separate from the rest of the form and is not checked by default. SMS consent is optional and is not required to submit a property inquiry, showing request, or callback request.

If the SMS consent checkbox is not selected, SIDNI will not use that form submission as SMS opt-in consent and will not send SMS messages.

The public SIDNI property inquiry form and optional SMS opt-in flow is available here: Property Inquiry Form and Optional SMS Opt-In.

Transactional SMS messages may relate to a property inquiry, showing request, callback request, customer-care response, or related follow-up.

Message frequency varies based on the user's property inquiry, showing request, callback request, and related follow-up.

Message and data rates may apply.

Users may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

Users may request help at any time by replying HELP or contacting stevensabell@gmail.com.

No Text-to-Join or Keyword-Based Opt-In

SIDNI does not use text-to-join or keyword-based SMS opt-in for this campaign. Users do not opt in by texting START, SUBSCRIBE, or another keyword.

The only SMS opt-in method for this campaign is the public SIDNI property inquiry form with the optional, unchecked SMS consent checkbox.

Mobile Number Privacy

SIDNI does not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile phone numbers may be used only for communications related to the user's submitted property inquiry, showing request, callback request, customer-care request, or related follow-up.

Information Sharing

SIDNI may share submitted request information with the listing agent or authorized service personnel so they can respond to the user's property inquiry, showing request, callback request, or related follow-up.

SIDNI may use service providers that help operate the website, messaging system, hosting, logging, or related technical services. These service providers are used only as needed to operate the service.

SIDNI does not sell personal information. SIDNI does not share mobile phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Retention

SIDNI may retain submitted information and related service records for as long as reasonably necessary to process requests, provide follow-up, maintain business records, troubleshoot service issues, comply with legal obligations, and operate the service.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of record and operational need.

User Choices

Users may choose not to submit the public property inquiry form.

Users may choose not to select the optional SMS consent checkbox. If the SMS consent checkbox is not selected, SIDNI will not use that form submission as SMS opt-in consent and will not send SMS messages.

Users who have opted in to SMS messages may opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Users may request help by replying HELP or contacting stevensabell@gmail.com.

Security

SIDNI uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect submitted information. However, no website, application, messaging system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

SIDNI is intended for use by individuals seeking information about real estate listings. SIDNI is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised last updated date.
```
