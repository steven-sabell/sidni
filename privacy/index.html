SIDNI Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-05-08

SIDNI is a temporary MVP brand for an AI-powered real estate listing inquiry assistant. SIDNI helps a real estate listing agent respond to inbound property inquiries, collect request details, submit showing or callback requests, queue transactional SMS confirmations, and notify the listing agent.

This Privacy Policy explains how SIDNI collects, uses, and protects information when you contact the listing assistant about a property.

Information We Collect

When you contact SIDNI about a listed property, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address, if provided
  • The property you are asking about
  • Your requested showing time or callback preference
  • Messages or questions you submit about the property
  • Basic records of your inquiry and related follow-up

SIDNI only collects information needed to respond to your inbound property inquiry, submit your request to the listing agent, and support related follow-up.

How We Use Your Information

SIDNI may use your information to:

  • Respond to your property inquiry
  • Submit a showing request to the listing agent
  • Submit a callback request to the listing agent
  • Send transactional SMS confirmations or follow-up messages related to your request
  • Notify the listing agent about your inquiry
  • Maintain basic records for operational, support, and compliance purposes

SIDNI does not confirm showing appointments. Showing requests are not confirmed until the listing agent follows up with the final showing time.

SMS Communications

By contacting SIDNI about a listed property and voluntarily providing your phone number, you consent to receive transactional SMS messages related to your inbound property inquiry.

For more information about SIDNI's SMS consent process, view the SMS Consent and Opt-In Flow.

SMS messages may include:

  • Confirmation that your showing request was received
  • Confirmation that your callback request was sent to the listing agent
  • Follow-up about your property inquiry
  • Reminders that a showing request is pending agent confirmation
  • Customer-care responses related to your request

SIDNI SMS messages are transactional and related to your inbound property inquiry. SIDNI does not send cold outreach, purchased-list messaging, or marketing blasts.

Message frequency varies based on your requests and interactions. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. You may request help at any time by replying HELP.

No Sale or Marketing Sharing of Phone Numbers or SMS Consent

SIDNI does not sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

How We Share Information

SIDNI may share your inquiry information with the listing agent or authorized service providers only as needed to operate the service, respond to your inquiry, submit your request, send transactional messages, or maintain the system.

SIDNI may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, agents, or the service.

Service Boundaries

SIDNI is designed to collect, inform, route, and submit property-related requests to the listing agent.

SIDNI does not provide legal, financial, lending, school, safety, demographic, protected-class, neighborhood, offer, or negotiation advice. For those topics, SIDNI may redirect you to the listing agent or another appropriate professional.

Data Retention

SIDNI keeps inquiry records only as long as reasonably needed for operational, support, compliance, and business purposes, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Security

SIDNI uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
