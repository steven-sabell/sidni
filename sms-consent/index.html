SIDNI SMS Consent and Opt-In Flow

Last Updated: 2026-05-20

SIDNI is an AI-powered real estate listing inquiry assistant for inbound property inquiries. SIDNI helps collect property questions, showing requests, callback requests, and related contact details, then routes them to the listing agent for follow-up.

SIDNI does not confirm showing appointments. Final showing confirmation must come from the listing agent.

How Users Opt In

Users opt in to receive SMS messages from SIDNI by submitting a property inquiry, showing request, or callback request and voluntarily providing their mobile phone number.

Before submitting their request, users are shown an SMS consent disclosure explaining that messages are transactional, message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, users can reply STOP to opt out, and users can reply HELP for help.

Example Consent Disclosure

The following disclosure is shown to users before they submit a property inquiry, showing request, or callback request:

By submitting this form or contacting SIDNI about a listed property, you agree to receive transactional SMS messages from SIDNI related to your property inquiry, showing request, callback request, or related follow-up. Message frequency varies based on your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchasing any property, product, or service. View our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

Example Opt-In Form Flow

A user may submit a request through a form or assistant flow containing fields such as:

  • Name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address, if provided
  • Property of interest
  • Property question, showing request, or callback request details
  • Requested showing time or callback preference, if applicable

The user provides their information voluntarily to request property-related follow-up from the listing agent.

Types of SMS Messages

SIDNI may send transactional SMS messages such as:

  • Confirmation that a property inquiry was received
  • Confirmation that a showing request was submitted
  • Confirmation that a callback request was submitted
  • Follow-up related to a specific listed-property inquiry
  • Reminder that a showing request is pending listing-agent confirmation
  • Customer-care responses related to the user's request

SIDNI does not send cold outreach, purchased-list messaging, mass marketing, unrelated promotional blasts, or third-party promotional messages.

Example SMS Messages

SIDNI: Thanks for asking about [Property Address]. We received your property inquiry and will follow up with the listing information you requested. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

SIDNI: We received your showing request for [Property Address] on [Requested Date/Time]. This is not a confirmed appointment. The listing agent will follow up to confirm availability. Reply STOP to opt out.

SIDNI: We received your callback request about [Property Address]. The listing agent will contact you at the number provided. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on the user's property inquiry, showing request, callback request, and related follow-up.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out

Users may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

Help

Users may request help at any time by replying HELP or contacting stevensabell@gmail.com.

Mobile Number Privacy

SIDNI does not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
