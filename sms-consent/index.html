Service Description

```

SIDNI by Steven Joseph Sabell is a real estate listing inquiry assistant for inbound property inquiries. SIDNI helps consumers submit property questions, showing requests, callback requests, and related contact details to the listing agent for follow-up.

SIDNI sends transactional SMS messages only to users who submit the public property inquiry form on this page and actively opt in to SMS follow-up.

SIDNI does not confirm showing appointments. Final showing confirmation must come from the listing agent.

SIDNI does not send cold outreach, purchased-list messaging, unrelated marketing blasts, third-party promotional messages, or loan/financing offers.

How Users Opt In

Users opt in to receive transactional SMS messages from SIDNI by Steven Joseph Sabell by submitting the public property inquiry form on this page and actively selecting the SMS consent checkbox.

The SMS consent checkbox is separate from the rest of the form and is not checked by default. Users must actively select the checkbox before submitting their mobile phone number for SMS follow-up.

This campaign uses one SMS opt-in method only: the public property inquiry form on this page with an unchecked SMS consent checkbox.

Property Inquiry Form and SMS Consent

The form below is the public SIDNI property inquiry intake and SMS opt-in flow. The SMS consent language appears at the point where the user's mobile phone number is collected.

Thank you. Your property request has been received for review. SMS follow-up will only be sent if SMS consent was selected.

The SMS consent checkbox is unchecked by default and must be actively selected by the user before submitting the form.

Types of SMS Messages

Users who opt in may receive transactional SMS messages related to their submitted property request, such as:

  • Confirmation that a property inquiry was received.
  • Confirmation that a showing request was submitted.
  • Confirmation that a callback request was submitted.
  • Follow-up related to a specific listed-property inquiry.
  • Reminder that a showing request is pending listing-agent confirmation.
  • Customer-care responses related to the user's request.

Example SMS Messages

SIDNI by Steven Joseph Sabell: Thanks for asking about [Property Address]. We received your property inquiry and will follow up with the listing information you requested. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

SIDNI by Steven Joseph Sabell: We received your showing request for [Property Address] on [Requested Date/Time]. This is not a confirmed appointment. The listing agent will follow up to confirm availability. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

SIDNI by Steven Joseph Sabell: We received your callback request about [Property Address]. The listing agent will contact you at the number provided. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on the user's property inquiry, showing request, callback request, and related follow-up.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt Out

Users may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

Help

Users may request help at any time by replying HELP or contacting stevensabell@gmail.com.

Mobile Number Privacy

SIDNI does not sell, rent, or share phone numbers or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information, including phone numbers and SMS opt-in consent, will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

No Text-to-Join Opt-In

SIDNI does not use text-to-join or keyword-based SMS opt-in for this campaign. Users do not opt in by texting START, SUBSCRIBE, or another keyword.

The only SMS opt-in method for this campaign is the public property inquiry form on this page with the unchecked SMS consent checkbox.
```
